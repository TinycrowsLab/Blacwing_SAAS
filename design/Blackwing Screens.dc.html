The clock runs, the agent lanes scroll, the countdown drains, and the kill and emergency buttons work. Theme is set in Tweaks. Tokens and components are in Blackwing Design System.

Try it
In 3a, click a ring or a row, type the name, then press Stop now. In 3b, press and hold the red button for 1.5 seconds. Letting go cancels.
Why hold
A double tap can happen by accident in a pocket. Holding is deliberate and still takes under two seconds. The ring fills so you can see your progress.
Timing bars
Each step's bar is drawn against its target (2 s for egress, 10 s for sandboxes). The tick marks the target, so a slow halt is obvious.
